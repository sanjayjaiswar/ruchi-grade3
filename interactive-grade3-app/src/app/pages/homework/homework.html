Short a

Short a is the vowel sound heard in cat. Keep the sound quick instead of saying the letter name.

Short o

Short o is the vowel sound heard in hot. Listen to the first vowel in longer words.

hobbybottlelotsflockpocket

How to work out a spelling word

  1. Say the word slowly and listen to its first vowel sound.
  2. Compare that sound with cat for short a or hot for short o.
  3. Write the whole word carefully, including every consonant.
  4. Read it again to make sure the written word matches the spoken word.

How context gives the answer

The sentence gives both grammar and meaning clues. In “more ___ any other subject,” a comparison needs than. In “___ soil,” a describing word meaning slightly wet needs damp.

After filling a blank, read the whole sentence aloud. The answer should sound natural and make sense.

Animal group words

A collective noun names a group. The worksheet asks for the particular group word commonly paired with each animal.

herd of cows pod of whales colony of ants gaggle of geese school of fish swarm of bees pack of wolves bed of clams
