Where Eureka Math fits

Same Grade 3 standards destination; different curriculum path

Eureka Math was the previous curriculum. Classroom Mathematics California is the current core program inside i-Ready. Both can teach California Grade 3 mathematics standards, but they use different books, units, lesson sequences, language, pacing, and digital tools. Eureka remains available here only as a reference and source of extra practice—not as an i-Ready lesson map.

What the Grade 3 program actually contains

Verified publisher and California review information—separate from assumptions about the school’s pacing.

Student materials

Two-volume Grade 3 Student Worktext

The worktext is organized into units, lessons, and multi-day sessions. Digital access can include a digital worktext, Family Center, math tools, learning games, Fluency Flight, assigned practice, and assessments.

Teacher materials

Teacher Guide Volumes 1 and 2 + digital tools

California’s review identifies the two Teacher Guide volumes, Digital Teacher Toolbox, and Digital Success Central. Publisher materials also describe planning reports, centers, comprehension checks, family letters, and progression videos.

Where My Path fits

Personalized Instruction is online practice, not the printed syllabus

When licensed and assigned by the school, My Path gives individualized online lessons informed by student needs. A student may therefore see work above or below the current classroom lesson without changing the official Grade 3 curriculum.

Student access

A working school login does not automatically mean every i-Ready product is enabled

The school controls the student roster, licensed products, assigned content, and sign-in path.

1

Use the current school sign-in route

Start through the school’s Clever or district portal, not the publisher’s teacher password-recovery page. Old credentials can still authenticate while the available apps change by school year.

2

Check which Math tiles appear

Look for i-Ready Math, Classroom Mathematics California or Student Worktext, My Path / Personalized Instruction, Diagnostic / Inform, and Family Center.

3

Treat missing content as provisioning—not a password conclusion

If the identity works but the Math program is absent, the teacher or school must confirm the correct code, roster, license, or app assignment.

Access boundary

There is no direct personal or homeschool i-Ready software license

Curriculum Associates says accounts and software access are provided through schools or organizations. Public family resources exist, but they do not unlock a student’s assigned course.
