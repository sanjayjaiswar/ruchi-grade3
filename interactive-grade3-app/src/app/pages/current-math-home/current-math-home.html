Where Eureka Math fits

Same Grade 3 standards destination; different curriculum path

Eureka Math was the previous curriculum. Classroom Mathematics California is the current core program inside i-Ready. Both can teach California Grade 3 mathematics standards, but they use different books, units, lesson sequences, language, pacing, and digital tools. Eureka remains available here only as a reference and source of extra practice—not as an i-Ready lesson map.

How multiplication and division meaning develops

California moves from equal groups to number-line jumps, arrays, area, inverse relationships, and flexible decomposition. The views below explain that official progression; they are not an i-Ready lesson sequence. Hover or focus a view to replay how the model is built.

Where My Path fits

Personalized Instruction is online practice, not the printed syllabus

When licensed and assigned by the school, My Path gives individualized online lessons informed by student needs. A student may therefore see work above or below the current classroom lesson without changing the official Grade 3 curriculum.
