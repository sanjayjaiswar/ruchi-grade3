The drafts are therefore quarantined from the active Unit path pending authorized material-level validation.

The detailed source-readiness decision is recorded in `grade3-reading-source-readiness-audit.md`. ## Grade 3 Standards Baseline

The [California Grade 3 ELA/Literacy register](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/ela?dl=0&maxgrade=3&mingrade=3&order=0&page=0&perpage=50) contains 44 numbered records across six domains:

- Reading: Literature — RL.3.1–10;
- Reading: Informational Text — RI.3.1–10;
- Reading: Foundational Skills — RF.3.3–4;
- Writing — W.3.1–10;
- Speaking and Listening — SL.3.1–6;
- Language — L.3.1–6.

RL.3.8 is explicitly not applicable to literature and W.3.9 explicitly begins in Grade 4. They remain in the CDE register and are shown as such rather than silently deleted or rewritten as Grade 3 expectations.

These standards are the Grade 3 learning expectations, not the adopted unit sequence. The portal therefore presents them as a standards reference beside the Benchmark year path, not as six invented curriculum modules.

## Reading-Level Interpretation

California’s RL.3.10 and RI.3.10 define the Grade 3 year-end outcome as independent and proficient comprehension at the high end of the Grades 2–3 text-complexity band. The [California ELA/ELD Framework text-complexity table](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/rl/cf/documents/elaeldfwchapter2.pdf) gives an updated Grades 2–3 Lexile text range of 420L–820L as one quantitative measure, but also explains that text complexity uses multiple measures.

The portal keeps the following concepts separate:

- a publisher Lexile label describes the difficulty of a selection;
- a learner Lexile measure would describe reader ability;
- Moreland’s SRI result is an assessment result;
- F&P is a different text-gradient and running-record system;
- none of those is interchangeable with the official Benchmark unit sequence.

No learner score, placement, conversion, or target is inferred from selection labels or from a prior-year report.

## Copyright And Practice Boundary

The public scope is sufficient to show official curriculum coordinates and metadata. It is not permission to reproduce copyrighted Benchmark readings, Teacher Edition directions, assessments, or answer keys.

Therefore the app:

- names the publisher unit, week, selection, genre, publisher Lexile label, and weekly focus;
- links the source beside official claims;
- tells the learner to open the named selection in the school-provided Benchmark materials;
- keeps daily practice drafts outside the active Unit path until material-level validation is possible;
- does not invent or reproduce an publisher-listed selection, official lesson title, official lesson text, answer, score, or placement.

## Public-Evidence Limit

The public evidence establishes Moreland’s adopted program and a publisher-produced Grade 3 unit/week/selection/focus sequence aligned by edition year. It does not prove Baker’s current order or expose the complete copyrighted Teacher Edition. A current Baker pacing artifact and authorized student/teacher materials are required before publishing daily activities as validated learning work.
