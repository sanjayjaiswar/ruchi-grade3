## Official Text-Evidence Practice Source

A public school-district copy of Benchmark Education Company’s **Grade 3 Text Evidence Questions for Texts for Close Reading** supplies ten printed question pages—one for each of the ten verified units—and ten questions per unit. All 100 questions were visually reconciled to the page images and mapped to the exact unit, week, selection role, and selection title in the 70-title publisher register.

This source controls the question wording and selection alignment. It does **not** supply the student passages, answer key, score, daily lesson order, or Baker classroom pacing. The active practice route therefore requires the learner’s school Benchmark text and adds only a clearly labeled portal-authored teaching layer. That layer uses progressive reader modeling, strategy-specific tap moves, spoken response frames, and tap-to-confirm self-checks; it never supplies a Benchmark answer or asks the learner to type one.

## Teaching-Layer Audit

The curriculum source and the teaching interaction are deliberately separated:

- **Official-content authority:** Benchmark controls the unit, selection, and question facts; Moreland and Baker establish local program context; California controls standards and state-system claims.
- **Instructional-sequence authority:** the [IES/REL Toolkit for Teaching K–3 Reading Comprehension](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/rel/reading-comprehension-k-3/teacher-pd-module1) supports explicit explanation and modeling followed by guided and independent use.
- **Product-interaction research:** elementary literacy products were reviewed for interaction mechanics only. Adopted mechanics are progressive disclosure, large tap targets, visible selected state, and oral response with tap-to-confirm completion.
- **No answer invention:** every modeled example is original supplemental portal material, visibly labeled, and independent of the Benchmark selection. It teaches a reading move without asserting passage facts or answering the official question.

The resulting practice route is Reading-native rather than a copy of the Math Blank/Solved pattern: Learn introduces the reading move, Watch reveals a reader's reasoning one step at a time, Practice uses three tap-first moves in the school text, and Respond asks the learner to say a concise evidence-based answer aloud. The entire learner path is completable without a keyboard.
