## Material Limitations

- The public source does not establish Baker’s current pacing.
- It does not prove the exact dates, classroom order within a week, teacher directions, daily lesson names, assessment forms, or which edition assets Ruchika currently sees in class.
- A newer 2022 Benchmark Advance edition exists and uses a different sequence. The portal is intentionally tied to the 2017 California-edition sequence because Moreland’s public adoption year is 2017–18; this is a reasoned edition match, not direct proof of every current classroom material.
- The public scope contains three week rows per unit but not fifteen official daily lessons per unit.
- The 150 portal practices were written in this repository. **Original supplemental practice—not a Benchmark, Baker, or Moreland lesson.** Structural checks cannot validate their educational fidelity. They are retained as drafts but removed from the active Unit path until each can be checked against authorized school materials.

## Required Evidence For A Stronger Claim

The edition-matched Grade 3 Teacher Resource System, Texts for Close Reading/student materials, and any lesson-referenced teacher/student resources are required before daily lesson work. Matching assessment and scoring materials are separately required before official-looking assessment practice. A current Baker Grade 3 pacing calendar, family unit letter, or authorized course view is additionally required to claim exact current classroom timing.
